# Vision

Build the world's most thoughtful platform for intentional personal change.

Ondenna helps people make meaningful progress by focusing on one important change at a time.

---

# Mission

Most people try to change everything at once.

Ondenna exists to help people change one thing intentionally, consistently and sustainably through 28-day seasons.

---

# Product Philosophy

Ondenna is not a habit tracker.

Ondenna is not a productivity app.

Ondenna is not a task manager.

Ondenna is a seasonal life system.

The goal is not to complete more tasks.

The goal is to become a different person through consistent action.

---

# Core Principles

## One Season

A season always lasts 28 days.

---

## One Focus

Every season has one primary focus.

Never multiple major goals.

---

## Progress over Perfection

Missing one day does not end the season.

Consistency matters more than perfection.

---

## Reflection

Every season should teach something.

Reflection is part of the product.

---

## Calm Interface

The interface should reduce stress.

Every screen should feel calm.

---

## Mobile First

The primary experience is mobile.

Desktop exists as a secondary experience.

---

# Target Users

# UX Principles

The user should never feel overwhelmed.

Every screen should answer one question only.

One primary action per screen.

The application should be usable in under one minute per day.

The product should encourage users to leave the app and live their lives.

---

# Success Metrics

Users complete seasons.

Users intentionally continue their current season.

Users start another season.

User wellbeing always has priority over engagement.

Users feel calmer after opening the app.
