\# Ondenna Copywriting Guide



Version: 1.0



This document defines the writing style, voice, and language rules for every piece of text inside Ondenna.



Every sentence in the product must follow these principles.



If a future copy conflicts with this document, this document takes precedence.



\---



\# Purpose



Words are part of the interface.



The goal of our writing is not to motivate.



The goal is to create clarity.



Users should never feel judged.



Users should never feel manipulated.



Users should always feel respected.



\---



\# Brand Voice



Ondenna speaks like a calm, thoughtful companion.



Not like a coach.



Not like a teacher.



Not like a productivity guru.



Imagine someone sitting beside you with a cup of tea.



They don't tell you what to do.



They simply help you think.



\---



\# Tone



Our tone is:



\- Calm

\- Warm

\- Honest

\- Clear

\- Quiet

\- Human

\- Intentional



Never:



\- Loud

\- Salesy

\- Pushy

\- Excited

\- Competitive

\- Dramatic

\- Overly emotional



\---



\# Sentence Style



Prefer short sentences.



Prefer simple words.



Avoid unnecessary adjectives.



Avoid long explanations.



Good:



Begin when you're ready.



Bad:



Let's start your incredible productivity journey today!



\---



\# Reading Level



Every sentence should be understandable in under 3 seconds.



If a sentence needs to be read twice,



rewrite it.



\---



\# Encouragement



Encouragement should feel gentle.



Good:



You can always begin again.



Today's enough.



One step at a time.



Bad:



You've got this!



Crush your goals!



Never give up!



\---



\# Productivity Language



Avoid productivity buzzwords.



Never use:



\- Hustle

\- Grind

\- Optimize

\- Maximize

\- Dominate

\- Perform

\- Unlock your potential

\- Peak performance



Prefer:



\- Reflect

\- Notice

\- Begin

\- Continue

\- Pause

\- Focus

\- Observe



\---



\# Seasons



Always call them:



Season



Never:



Challenge



Program



Mission



Journey



Sprint



Campaign



\---



\# Daily Actions



Preferred words:



Begin Season



Continue



Reflect



Check In



Finish Reflection



Save



Cancel



Go Back



Done



Avoid:



Execute



Complete Mission



Claim



Level Up



Unlock



\---



\# Empty States



Every empty state should answer three questions:



What is happening?



Why?



What can I do next?



Example:



No active season.



When you're ready, begin your first season.



\[Begin Season]



\---



\# Error Messages



Errors should never blame the user.



Bad:



Invalid input.



Something went wrong.



Error 500.



Good:



We couldn't save your season.



Please try again.



\---



\# Success Messages



Keep success quiet.



Good:



Season created.



Reflection saved.



Reminder updated.



Bad:



Amazing!



Fantastic!



Congratulations!



You did it!



\---



\# Confirmation Dialogs



Keep confirmations clear.



Good:



Delete this season?



This cannot be undone.



Buttons:



Delete



Cancel



Avoid:



Are you absolutely sure you want to permanently remove this amazing season forever?



\---



\# Buttons



Buttons should use verbs.



Good:



Begin Season



Continue



Save



Delete



Cancel



Go Back



Bad:



Yes



Next



Okay



Submit



Click Here



\---



\# Notifications



Notifications should feel optional.



Good:



Your daily reflection is ready.



Bad:



Don't forget!



You're falling behind!



Come back now!



\---



\# Questions



Questions should invite reflection.



Good:



What do you want to change this season?



Why is this important to you?



How did today feel?



Bad:



What's your goal?



How productive were you?



Did you succeed?



\---



\# Numbers



Avoid percentages whenever possible.



Prefer:



Day 8 of 28



Instead of:



29% Complete



\---



\# Dates



Use natural language.



Good:



Today



Tomorrow



Monday



March 12



Avoid technical formatting.



\---



\# Humor



Avoid jokes.



Avoid sarcasm.



Avoid memes.



Reflection should feel timeless.



\---



\# Emoji



Do not use emojis inside the application.



The interface should communicate through typography, spacing and icons.



\---



\# Capitalization



Use sentence case.



Good:



Begin season



Reflection saved



Choose a start date



Bad:



BEGIN SEASON



Begin Season Now!



\---



\# Punctuation



Prefer periods.



Avoid multiple exclamation marks.



Never use:



!!!



???



...



Unless absolutely necessary.



\---



\# AI Rules



Before generating any UI text:



1\. Read this document.

2\. Read design-language.md.

3\. Prefer shorter copy.

4\. Remove unnecessary words.

5\. Avoid marketing language.

6\. Avoid productivity language.

7\. Respect the user's attention.

8\. If two versions are equally clear, choose the shorter one.



\---



\# Copy Review Checklist



Before any text is accepted, ask:



\- Is this calm?

\- Is this clear?

\- Is this respectful?

\- Is this necessary?

\- Can it be shorter?

\- Would this still make sense if half the words were removed?



If the answer is yes,



rewrite it.



\---



\# Examples



\## Welcome



Good:



One season.



One intentional change.



Bad:



Welcome to the world's best productivity app!



\---



\## CTA



Good:



Begin season



Bad:



Let's do this!



\---



\## Reflection Saved



Good:



Reflection saved.



Bad:



Amazing work! You're making incredible progress!



\---



\## Reminder



Good:



Today's reflection is ready.



Bad:



You haven't checked in today!



\---



\## Empty Dashboard



Good:



Nothing for today.



Take your time.



Bad:



You're missing today's tasks!



\---



\# Final Principle



Every sentence should leave the user feeling calmer than before they read it.



If a sentence creates pressure,



rewrite it.



If a sentence creates guilt,



delete it.



If a sentence feels like marketing,



start over.
